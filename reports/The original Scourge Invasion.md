# The original Scourge Invasion

In patch 1.11, players defended a zone by destroying every invading necropolis. Each necropolis depended on three ground camps. At each camp, players killed undead to weaken a shard, then spent Necrotic Runes to interrupt its repair. Clearing the zone earned one realm victory, and the 150th victory ended outdoor attacks. A faithful implementation needs to remember which particular camps fell and to apply the original three-week deadline separately to vendors, quest givers and bonus dungeon bosses. ([June 2006 player accounts](https://lurkerlounge.com/forums/printthread.php?tid=4312), [ClassicDB spawn data](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/blob/13e933726f386399d9820138fbd1123e7cda86c8/Full_DB/ClassicDB_1_12_1_z2815.sql.gz), [Blizzard’s comparison of 2006 and 2020](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/naxxramas-opening-time-and-scourge-invasion-adjustments/741200/1))

## How players destroyed a shard

Each camp fielded a mix of two undead types drawn from ghouls, skeletons and ghosts. In the installed data, six ordinary and rare enemy templates already cast Zap Necrotic Crystal (28032) on death. Route that effect once to the camp’s intact shard (16136). When depleted, it becomes a Damaged Necrotic Shard (16172) with four Cultist Engineers (16230) channeling repairs. June 2006 players describe seeing the crystal weaken as they killed nearby undead and seeing engineers restore its health. ([Launch-week account](https://lurkerlounge.com/forums/printthread.php?tid=4312), [ClassicDB data](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/blob/13e933726f386399d9820138fbd1123e7cda86c8/Full_DB/ClassicDB_1_12_1_z2815.sql.gz), [CMaNGOS shard and wave scripts](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/28a26c388a3c817f34cdb4737847a345841b6a08/src/game/AI/ScriptDevAI/scripts/world/scourge_invasion.cpp#L745-L910))

A player used eight Necrotic Runes (22484) on one engineer to summon one Shadow of Doom (16143). The gameplay path is gossip menu 7166, option 0, condition 3745 and Summon Boss (31315). Killing the Shadow triggers zap 28056. Four Shadows killed promptly can finish a fresh damaged shard. A contemporary player saw repairs prolong the fight. CMaNGOS assigns each Shadow one quarter of maximum shard health and uses an hourly refresh as a placeholder. The original repair rate remains unknown. On completion, the reconstruction applies Soul Revival to nearby players and relays the camp’s destruction to its necropolis. ([2006 battle account](https://www.lurkerlounge.com/forums/thread-4268-post-110619.html), [Summon and rune payment](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/28a26c388a3c817f34cdb4737847a345841b6a08/src/game/AI/ScriptDevAI/scripts/world/scourge_invasion.cpp#L174-L201), [Shard and repair handling](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/28a26c388a3c817f34cdb4737847a345841b6a08/src/game/AI/ScriptDevAI/scripts/world/scourge_invasion.cpp#L638-L897))

## The world database fixes the locations

ClassicDB z2815 places 14 necropolis anchors (16401), 14 health controllers (16421), 42 summoning circles (game object 181136), 42 relays (16386), 42 proxies (16398) and 1,129 minion finders (16356). Each health controller has three assigned circles. Events 90 and 91 supply three necropolises each in Winterspring and Tanaris. Events 92–95 supply two each in Azshara, Blasted Lands, Eastern Plaguelands and Burning Steppes respectively. Reuse each spawn GUID, including the six capital Mouths of Kel’Thuzad. Create shards, engineers, waves and Shadows as encounter actors. Casting 27866 to recreate the placed controllers would duplicate them. The audited integration surface spans thirteen NPC templates sharing nine script names, two game-object bindings and the 31315 spell callback. ([Pinned ClassicDB data](https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db/blob/13e933726f386399d9820138fbd1123e7cda86c8/Full_DB/ClassicDB_1_12_1_z2815.sql.gz), [CMaNGOS reference registrations](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/28a26c388a3c817f34cdb4737847a345841b6a08/src/game/AI/ScriptDevAI/scripts/world/scourge_invasion.cpp))

## Realm victories and the event deadline

A June 2006 observer saw all six zones under attack at launch. CMaNGOS models later attacks with at most two active zones when starting a replacement and randomized 45–60 minute delays. These later scheduling rules are reconstruction choices. The client displays remaining necropolises and an attack icon per zone. Realm world state 2219 increases once when the last ship in a zone falls. Both newly logged-in and already connected players need the resulting map state. ([June 2006 observation](https://wowpedia.fandom.com/wiki/Talk%3AScourge_Invasion#Some_notes), [Reference scheduling](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/28a26c388a3c817f34cdb4737847a345841b6a08/src/game/World/WorldState.cpp#L1501-L1552), [Client fields](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/28a26c388a3c817f34cdb4737847a345841b6a08/src/game/World/WorldStateDefines.h#L137-L152))

Original realm milestones at 50, 100 and 150 victories unlocked successive Marks of the Dawn in the capitals. After 150, outdoor attacks ceased for that activation. Vendors, quest givers and extra dungeon bosses had a three-week window from launch. The 2020 Classic event used 100, 200 and 300 wins, a four-week window and an additional week of attacks after the final milestone. ClassicDB separates the main event (17), dungeon spawns (89), milestone populations (96–98) and aftermath vendors (99). ([Blizzard’s original and Classic rules](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/naxxramas-opening-time-and-scourge-invasion-adjustments/741200/1), [Reference milestone transitions](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/28a26c388a3c817f34cdb4737847a345841b6a08/src/game/World/WorldState.cpp#L1248-L1283))

## What a passing implementation should demonstrate

Exercise the normal kill and gossip paths. With seven runes the player keeps all seven. With eight, one player pays exactly once and one Shadow appears, even if two players select the same engineer concurrently. Confirm that a real Shadow death damages only its shard and that three distinct completed camps remove exactly their assigned ship. Replay the final death across a node handoff and a restart. The adjacent ship must keep its progress, defeated camps must stay cleared, and exhausting the zone must credit one victory on that realm alone. Repeat with two realms sharing local spawn GUIDs. ([Gossip callback](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/28a26c388a3c817f34cdb4737847a345841b6a08/src/game/AI/ScriptDevAI/scripts/world/scourge_invasion.cpp#L934-L989), [dmangos realm progress precedent](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/world/wareffort.d#L694-L880))

Check 49→50, 99→100 and 149→150 with clients on separate nodes and a late login. Restore a pending zone-start timer after victory 150 and verify that outdoor attacks stay ended. With an early final victory, check dungeon access, quest givers and vendors on day 20 and at the three-week deadline. Together these checks distinguish persistent realm progression from a camp that merely looks correct to one nearby player. ([Blizzard’s original event rules](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/naxxramas-opening-time-and-scourge-invasion-adjustments/741200/1))
