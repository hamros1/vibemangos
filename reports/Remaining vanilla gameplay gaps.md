# Vanilla gameplay audit

## Implementation status

The audit below describes revision `780ff19`. **The remaining confirmed difference is what triggers Gluth’s Decimate.** The damage effect now brings affected players and Zombie Chow down to 5% of maximum health and leaves a target already below 5% alone. The encounter still schedules the cast at 105 seconds. Blizzard ruled out a timer and did not publish the positive trigger. The follow-up checked 41 complete Classic Era event streams. The longest covered 104.603 seconds and none recorded the cast. These are server and protocol regression results. Original-client checks still worth doing include the hunter training window for a wrong-family lesson, the opposing-faction Inspect menu and the presentation of realm names on the battleground scoreboard. The stock client's behavior for those UI details remains unconfirmed.

The release check passed with 272 unit-test modules, 1,983 player spells checked against game data, and a two-node run with 250 bots, 66 handoffs and zero crashes. The NPC sweep still lists 180 previously recorded limitations in the wider emulator. A separate two-realm run exercised matchmaking and return travel using real protocol clients.

## Findings at revision 780ff19


**Two gaps affect ordinary play on a clustered realm.** A shaman can regain spent Lightning Shield charges by crossing a node boundary. Two eligible party members standing a few yards apart can lose the ability to share a leveling quest across that same boundary. The largest encounter gaps change what players actually do to win: Heigan lacks the dance, Sapphiron lacks the lethal breath and shelter, and players break Razorgore’s eggs themselves. Alterac Valley and the Scourge Invasion also have data and partial scripts for activities whose progression is missing. [Aura persistence](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/world/characterdb.d#L421) · [Quest sharing](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/world/questhandlers.d#L1156) · [Sapphiron](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/scripts/naxxramas.d#L1893) · [Alterac Valley](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/world/alterac.d#L22)

**Scope and confidence.** This audit samples late vanilla, targeting the original 1.12 client and using Classic Era accounts of the original mechanics where appropriate. The findings come from tracing executable paths at commit `780ff19`, reading installed Classic-DB and client records, and checking existing tests against patch notes, extracted client UI, Blizzard reference-client findings and encounter guides. No new live gameplay trials were run. The previous real-data sweep recorded 1,983 passing player-spell casts. Its checks cover selected cast outcomes. They cannot tell whether a second player sees a trap, an aura survives migration with the right charge count, or a raid survives by standing behind an ice block. The audit did not inventory every recipe, quest, item, event or encounter, so it cannot give a percentage of vanilla implemented. ## Alterac Valley and the Scourge Invasion lack event progression

**Alterac Valley has four distinct gaps.** Mounts and hides should unlock cavalry, mine supplies should unlock a ground assault, and rescued Wing Commanders and faction drops should unlock air support. The turn-in handler advances armor upgrades and faction-lord offerings, leaving those three deployments unfinished. A fourth branch already summons Ivus or Lokholar, but does so immediately at 200 offerings. Classic players escort the summoners to the Field of Strife and complete a ten-player ritual. Test each assault from supply through deployment, then test the escort and ritual with nine and ten participants on both factions. [Classic battleground guide](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/guide/alterac-valley-battleground-strategy-wow-classic) · [Supported objectives](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/world/alterac.d#L22) · [Turn-ins](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/world/alterac.d#L282) · [Faction-lord summon](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/world/alterac.d#L490)

**The patch 1.11 Scourge Invasion has event data and support spells, but its zone encounter scripts are missing.** The installed database has the six zone events and victory milestones. Thirteen creature-template entries reference invasion scripts without registered handlers, including Necrotic Shard, Damaged Necrotic Shard, Cultist Engineer and Necropolis Relay. Activate the historical event in a test realm and check the complete transition from weakening a shard through clearing a necropolis and advancing realm progress once. Seeing an event spawn verifies only the first step. [Blizzard’s event description](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/ptr-scourge-invasion-test-is-underway/679484) · [Script registry](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/scripts/package.d#L82) · [Existing support spells](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/scripts/worldspells.d#L150)

**Cross-realm battleground matchmaking is absent.** This matters for a deployment operating multiple realms. Blizzard dates cross-realm battlegrounds to original patch 1.12. Here, players can queue across simulation nodes of the same realm, while realm isolation and the cluster handshake prevent a battlegroup from drawing players from two realms. An acceptance test needs two separate realms forming one match, including characters with colliding local names or GUIDs, correct rewards, and a return to each home realm. [Blizzard’s Classic FAQ](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23090136) · [Realm isolation](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/docs/CLUSTER.md#L788) · [Handshake](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/cluster/link.d#L523) · [Queue key](https://github.com/hamros1/vibemangos/blob/780ff1948f923d735e4abef929b4d026e9278fee/source/dmangos/world/bgqueue.d#L190)

## Where to start

**Fix Lightning Shield persistence and party quest sharing first.** Both affect routine play and have inexpensive, decisive tests that compare the same interaction on one node and across a handoff or boundary. Pair hunter-trap visibility and Disarm Trap next so their regression test covers the entire player interaction. For a first raid milestone, implement Sapphiron’s breath and ice-block shelter together. Keep cross-realm matchmaking as a separate deployment decision, and keep Gluth’s exact trigger open until reference evidence distinguishes competing explanations.
