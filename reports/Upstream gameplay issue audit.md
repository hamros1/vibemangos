# Upstream reports expose Classic gameplay bugs

The CMaNGOS and vMaNGOS trackers exposed gameplay problems in DMaNGOS ranging from missing raid effects to incorrect quest rewards, class mechanics, and rare spawns. The audit started from `0615d15` and used the emulator's D code, the installed Classic-DB, and the 1.12 client data to check reports. The CMaNGOS inventory covered **761 reports** with the `Expansion: Classic (1.12)` label, including 168 open and 593 closed, with selected gameplay reports investigated in depth. The vMaNGOS review screened **370 open and closed reports** dated April 7, 2024 through November 22, 2026, and made a separate pass through the first 100 reports with the `spell` label. Those samples can overlap. Investigation prioritized reports that identified a concrete spell, quest, creature, or encounter. [CMaNGOS Classic tracker](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues?q=label%3A%22Expansion%3A+Classic+%281.12%29%22), [vMaNGOS tracker](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues?q=is%3Aissue), [vMaNGOS spell reports](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues?q=is%3Aissue+label%3Aspell)

## Three encounters gain their missing spell effects

**Firewalker’s Fire Blossom** was missing its periodic damage spell. Each pulse now picks a living player on the Firewalker's threat list. The original Molten Core raid guide describes random targets and notes that successive pulses can hit the same player. **Baron Rivendare's Unholy Aura** had the same missing trigger in DMaNGOS. Its damage pulse now hits players without putting them in combat on its own. [CMaNGOS #4116](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/4116), [Amazon Basin Molten Core guide](https://www.theamazonbasin.com/wiki/index.php/Horde_Molten_Core_Guide), [vMaNGOS #3609](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/3609), [vMaNGOS Unholy Aura fix](https://github.com/vmangos/core/commit/5212a8e), [DMaNGOS periodic spell routing](../source/dmangos/world/spellauras.d)

**Kurinnaxx's Sand Trap** cast had no handler to create the trap. It now creates one visible trap at a randomly selected eligible player's location and springs it there after four seconds. The vMaNGOS report documents the one-trap behavior, and CMaNGOS implements the four-second delay. [vMaNGOS #3579](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/3579), [CMaNGOS Kurinnaxx encounter](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/master/src/game/AI/ScriptDevAI/scripts/kalimdor/ruins_of_ahnqiraj/boss_kurinnaxx.cpp), [DMaNGOS spell effects](../source/dmangos/world/spelleffects.d), [Raid regression](../source/dmangos/world/realscripts_raids.d)

## Regeneration, threat, and energy follow their spell rules

**Troll's Blood and Demon Armor** applied their advertised five-second health regeneration amounts every second. The rate now uses the five-second unit, with fractional health carried across the server's regeneration ticks. Grip of Command and Aura of Battle retain their explicitly documented per-second rates. **Net** now halves the victim's threat on its caster through the triggered Net Guard aura. **Relentless Strikes** formerly lost its energy return when the finishing move killed its target. A lethal five-point finisher can now return 25 energy to the rogue. [CMaNGOS #3586](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3586), [Major Troll's Blood tooltip](https://classicdb.ch/?item=20004), [Demon Armor tooltip](https://classicdb.ch/?spell=11735), [vMaNGOS #2737](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/2737), [vMaNGOS #2331](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/2331)

**Searing Totem** now targets hostile creatures fighting its owner, a group member, or a pet or guardian belonging to either. Totems also no longer draw aggro by proximity alone. The owner-death check covers behavior already present in the emulator. Player-owned totems disappear when their owner dies, while NPC-owned totems can remain for their own spawn duration. [vMaNGOS #2862](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/2862), [vMaNGOS #2863](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/2863), [vMaNGOS #2864](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/2864), [DMaNGOS totem regression](../source/dmangos/world/realclass_paladin_shaman_druid.d)

## Classic-DB now drives the intended quests and rare spawns

**Blacksmiths choosing their first specialization** receive a referral from Myolor or Krathok and learn the specialty through the trainer quest. Classic-DB already contains the lessons, and the server supplies the missing hidden reputation requirements for those quests while respecting explicit requirements in the database. The selector also lets veterans relearn the specialty they previously earned through its quest. **Grey quests** continue to reduce reputation gains and apply the full reputation penalty at default rates. In the reported example, a level-60 player's Strange Alliance turn-in costs the full 500 Magram reputation. [vMaNGOS #3451](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/3451), [Specialization quest footage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qoHGKYkbrWM&t=789s), [vMaNGOS #3387](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/3387), [DMaNGOS quest data](../source/dmangos/world/quests.d)

**Mistress Nagmara** starts her scene when a player selects her gossip option after turning in The Love Potion. **Blackwood food stores** now use ordinary chest looting, removing the extra warrior that the server could summon when the nearest guard was absent. The separate bonfire event still summons its own warriors. **Akubar the Seer** has eight possible placements in Classic-DB and an existing pool that limits him to one active spawn. The loader now reads creature-entry pool rules so the imported pool can enforce that limit. [vMaNGOS #3583](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/3583), [vMaNGOS #3444](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/3444), [Classic Blackwood food-store footage](https://youtu.be/1DVeKSMy2m0?t=4867), [CMaNGOS #3686](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3686), [CMaNGOS pool loader](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/master/src/game/Pools/PoolManager.cpp#L662), [DMaNGOS pool loader](../source/dmangos/world/worlddata.d)

## Follow-up fixes

**NPC Arcane Missiles** now deals periodic damage to the original target. Switching aggro leaves the channel attached to that target. Interrupts stop subsequent launches and apply the Arcane school lockout. The real spell tests also exercise a projectile landing on another node. **Furious Howl** now adds its physical damage bonus to queued weapon attacks. **Sweeping Strikes** copies the damage that passes through a shield and keeps its charge when the shield absorbs the entire hit. [CMaNGOS #3461](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3461), [vMaNGOS #3172](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/3172), [vMaNGOS #2654](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/2654)

**Akubar's spawn pool** now saves the selected placement and deadline per realm. The current owner advances the choice, and a revision check rejects stale transitions. Tests cover a standalone restart, a clean handoff, and recovery from an expired owner. **Molten Core trash** now uses the imported respawn delays and respects the data's boss links. Garr remains dead until the raid reset, and his Lava Surgers stay down after his death. [CMaNGOS #3686](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3686), [CMaNGOS #4117](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/4117), [CMaNGOS #4118](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/4118), [Amazon Basin Molten Core guide](https://www.theamazonbasin.com/wiki/index.php/Horde_Molten_Core_Guide)

**Magmadar** now casts one Lava Bomb per cycle and each landed bomb creates a Conflagration patch. The tracker report cites 2005 footage in support of the single timer. The 12–15-second interval follows the published encounter implementation, and the exact vanilla interval and spell-variant selection remain uncertain. The 1.12 data supplies both variants and the patch effect. **Jeziba** turns toward the catalogue, speaks, points and returns to her original facing. **The Battle of Darrowshire** now leads to Joseph's quest credit and his reunion with Pamela, followed by the Hidden Treasures reward. [vMaNGOS #2648](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/2648), [TrinityCore's Magmadar script](https://trinitycore.net/d0/d26/boss__magmadar_8cpp_source), [vMaNGOS #3587](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/3587), [vMaNGOS #2999](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/2999)

The follow-up reproduced two kills of each of the four reported Dark Strand enemies with The Tower of Althalaxx active. Each respawn generated loot and the client packet opened the corpse on both kills. The Sarltooth report still lacks a verified patrol path, and the Shadowforge Cache report leaves the expected behavior unspecified. [CMaNGOS #4024](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/4024), [CMaNGOS #3850](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3850), [CMaNGOS #3588](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3588)

Source and data checks already cover several reported symptoms. Hunter pet bonus stamina adds ten health per point in DMaNGOS, a stack merged into the bank leaves its excess in the source slot, and Chain Heal already prioritizes eligible group members by health deficit. [CMaNGOS #4051](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/4051), [CMaNGOS #3623](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3623), [vMaNGOS #3059](https://github.com/vmangos/core/issues/3059)

## Verification

The initial fixes passed 272 test modules with the installed Classic-DB and 1.12 client data. The timed trap regression covers the four-second fuse in the raid and preservation of the remaining fuse during an object handoff. Automated client-packet walkthroughs passed for Alliance Armorsmith, Horde Weaponsmith, and Mistress Nagmara's quest turn-in and gossip sequence. The initial two-node regression finished with all 250 bots connected, 138 handoffs, and zero crashes or lease failures.

The follow-up passed the full release build, all 272 test modules with Classic-DB, and both spell sweeps with zero new failures across 1,983 player spells and 3,277 NPC spell cases. The two-node regression finished with all 250 bots connected, 14 splits, 4 merges, 61 handoffs, and zero crashes or lease failures. The world-data update was applied twice and left one set of scene rows. Automated tests advanced Akubar's timer and exercised the database race. A full-length live respawn and game-client play remain unverified. [Existing installation instructions](../docs/SETUP.md)
