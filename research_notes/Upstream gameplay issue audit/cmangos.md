# CMaNGOS 1.12 tracker audit

## Which Classic issues carried over?

### Takeaway

The paginated GitHub inventory contained 761 reports under `Expansion: Classic (1.12)`, with 168 open and 593 closed. I prioritized gameplay reports with a concrete spell, item, creature, or encounter and compared selected reports with the D server at `0615d15` and its installed ClassicDB data. [Tracker label](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues?q=label%3A%22Expansion%3A+Classic+%281.12%29%22), [GitHub issue inventory](https://api.github.com/repos/cmangos/issues/issues?state=all&labels=Expansion%3A%20Classic%20%281.12%29&per_page=100)

### Cited Findings

- **Firewalker, open #4116.** The installed ClassicDB event `1166602` casts Fire Blossom (`19636`) on Firewalker (`11666`). The input spell roots the caster and triggers hostile fire spell `19637` each second, but the original D creature aura tick did not dispatch periodic triggers. The Amazon Basin's Horde Molten Core guide describes random targets and occasional repeated hits on the same player. [Issue #4116](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/4116), [Horde Molten Core guide](https://www.theamazonbasin.com/wiki/index.php/Horde_Molten_Core_Guide), [1.12 spell data](https://classicdb.ch/?spell=19636), [Creature aura updates](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/creature.d:1014), [Periodic spell handling](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/spellauras.d)
- **Combat health regeneration, open #3586.** The D player implementation read the aura's raw amount as health per second. Classic tooltips for Major Troll's Blood, Weak Troll's Blood, and Demon Armor specify a five-second rate. The branch now applies the rate across ordinary two-second ticks and carries fractional health into the following tick. Grip of Command and Aura of Battle have explicit per-second tooltips and retain those rates. [Issue #3586](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3586), [Major Troll's Blood](https://classicdb.ch/?item=20004), [Weak Troll's Blood](https://classicdb.ch/?spell=3219), [Demon Armor](https://classicdb.ch/?spell=11735), [Grip of Command](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/cn/spell=21172), [Aura of Battle](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=21174/aura-of-battle), [Real spell regression](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/realrules_world2.d)
- **NPC Arcane Missiles, open #3461.** A CMaNGOS contributor independently reproduced the reported NPC channel failure. Local Scarlet Invoker (`1835`) actually uses rank `15735`, which has a periodic caster aura that should fire `15736` at the enemy. Darkmaster Gandling uses the same-shaped `15790` to `15791`, and `22272` to `22273` appears in EventAI for NPC `12557`. The D player's Arcane Missiles already store their channel target. At the baseline, the creature channel pipeline supported scripted nearby-creature targets while ordinary enemy-target periodic channels had no equivalent. [Issue #3461](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3461), [Player tick targeting](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/aurahandlers.d:998), [Creature channel selection](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/spelldamage.d:337), [Gandling AI](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/scripts/scholomance.d:313)
- **Akubar the Seer, open #3686.** The installed ClassicDB already groups all eight placements of rare NPC `8298` through `pool_creature_template` pool `1031`, with a maximum of one active spawn. D previously loaded only GUID-based creature pools. The fix also imports entry-based memberships, with a specific GUID assignment taking precedence over a template assignment. This installation has 208 template entries in 207 pools covering 830 placements. No placement is also in a GUID-based creature pool or a creature spawn group. [Issue #3686](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3686), [2005 World of Warcraft atlas](https://ptgmedia.pearsoncmg.com/imprint_downloads/brady/connected/wowatlas/RegionalIndices_hr.pdf), [CMaNGOS pool import](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/master/src/game/Pools/PoolManager.cpp#L662), [Local spawn selection](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/worlddata.d:968)

### Inferences

- Missing creature periodic trigger execution explains Firewalker and the NPC Arcane Missiles gap. Fire Blossom chooses a living attacker each tick. Arcane Missiles needs the original cast target and channel interruption state for its entire sequence.
- Loading the imported pool preserves ClassicDB's individual positions and respawn intervals. The cluster uses the realm's shared startup seed when choosing from these pools. [Cluster spawn seed](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/main.d:158)
- Follow-up testing confirmed a separate ownership bug. At the old placement's respawn deadline, the local pool rolled an alternative and wrote its timer without publishing the new selection to the alternative's owner. A restart also discarded the in-memory choice. The server now stores the selection and deadline together per realm for single-choice overworld creature pools. The current owner advances the revision atomically, and other nodes read that selection before loading a new region and during normal respawn processing. Same-placement rolls materialize at the existing deadline. Alternate placements wait their own delay, matching the upstream implementation. [CMaNGOS pool update](https://github.com/cmangos/mangos-classic/blob/master/src/game/Pools/PoolManager.cpp#L385), [Persistent state](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/creaturepools.d), [World respawn regression](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/context.d)

### Gaps

- The inventory counts are for all label matches. The work compared a selected subset against the installation and did not replay each report in the game client.
- The historical atlas supports Akubar's rare classification. I found no firsthand Classic report proving all eight recorded positions are used or the precise probability of choosing any one of them.
- The real-data regression checks one initially active Akubar and preservation of his configured death timer after a standalone restart. An isolated world test exercises split ownership, graceful transfer, fenced-owner recovery, and a destination restart with a shortened respawn timer. Two scratch MariaDB clients competed for the same initial claim and the same revision. Testing with the stock five-to-eight-hour schedule on a running cluster remains outstanding. [Real ClassicDB regression](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/realrules_world2.d), [World respawn regression](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/context.d), [Character database routing](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/characterdb.d)

## Which other reports are already covered or need more evidence?

### Takeaway

Several upstream symptoms have explicit local code or matching data. The raid respawn and movement-path reports merit isolated follow-up once encounter state or historical coordinates are available.

### Cited Findings

- **Open #4051 and #3817, hunter pet bonus stamina.** The local derived pet health already adds ten health for each point of stamina above the pet's base, without applying the reported first-twenty-point reduction to that bonus. [Issue #4051](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/4051), [Issue #3817](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3817), [Pet health formula](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/pet.d:479)

[Issue #3623](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3623), [Bank merge implementation](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/inventory.d:1702)
- **Open #3585 and #3584, Premeditation and Camouflage.** The installed `14183` data grants combo points before applying its retention aura. The local effect pipeline preserves effect order, and the aura expiration subtracts retained points. Camouflage has an existing real spell regression. [Issue #3585](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3585), [Issue #3584](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3584), [Spell effect ordering](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/spellhandlers.d:2380), [Retention expiration](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/aurahandlers.d:575), [Camouflage regression](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/realclass_warrior_rogue_hunter.d:1748)
- **Open #3453, Restorative Potion.** Item `9030` applies `11359`, which triggers `23396` every five seconds. The player periodic-trigger handler casts it on its owner, and dispel type 7 includes magic, curse, disease, and poison with one effect removed per tick. This covers the generic report that the potion removes nothing. [Issue #3453](https://github.com/cmangos/issues/issues/3453), [Classic tooltip](https://classicdb.ch/?item=9030), [Player periodic trigger](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/aurahandlers.d:998), [Dispel selection](/home/hampus/dmangos/source/dmangos/world/spellauras.d:561)
